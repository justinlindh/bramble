# Bramble Pager v1 — Independent Hardware Review

**Scope reviewed:** all of `hardware/pager/v1/` on `pager-v1` plus firmware commits `a8fae76e`, `4002a523`, `5d62fa4d`. Bulk autorouting treated as out of scope; locked RF/USB routes, placement, schematic, docs, firmware, and enclosure in scope.

**Method:** independent `kicadxml` netlist export with per-pin verification, `pcbnew` geometry/track extraction, `kicad-cli` DRC, SVG renders read back visually, and datasheet cross-checks (FS8205A, ATGM336H, GDEY0213B74 p29, SX1262, TP4056). Firmware and enclosure reviewed in depth by two delegated agents; folded in below.

## BLOCKER

**B1. GNSS RF feed is 21mm and loops around the entire GPS module, 0.15mm from a digital pad.**
`/radio/GNSS_RF` measures 20.95mm (LoRa `/radio/RF_ANT` is 1.80mm for comparison). DRC flags a 14.4mm `GNSS_RF` segment at 0.1535mm to `U202` pad 16 (SDA). The render shows L201 (bias inductor) at the top of the module and ANT2 at the bottom, forcing the RF node down the module's left side, across the bottom, to RF_IN, then wrapping to ANT2. Violates the do-not-regress invariant (`DESIGN.md:245`, `README.md:95`: "RF trace: CPW, <5mm, no crossings") and the ATGM336H datasheet note p14 ("antenna interface as close as possible to the RF input pin... try not to go near high frequency, large amplitude digital signals"). Degrades GNSS sensitivity and couples digital noise into the 1.575GHz input; the 0.15mm-to-SDA is a DRC failure that bulk routing will not fix. **Fix:** co-locate ANT2, L201, and the module RF_IN corner; short (<5mm) direct ground-stitched CPW from ANT2 to pin 11 with L201 tapping the junction. Needs re-placement, so it must land before gerbers.

**B2. (Enclosure) GPS antenna bump collides with the e-paper module's right edge — case cannot assemble.**
`pager_case.scad:342-344,398-410`. Module outline occupies board-local x22..81.2; the GPS retaining wall (x80.5..81.9, z10.4..17.9) survives the sky-pocket cut (which hollows only x81.9..95.0), overlapping the module pocket void at x80.5..81.5, y27.5..42.5, z12.35..14.4 — exactly where the module's right corner seats. Space conflict: only 14.8mm remains between module edge (x81.2) and board edge (x96) but the GPS patch is 15mm + 1.4mm walls each side. **Fix:** shrink the GPS patch to ~12mm and push `GPS_POCKET_P0[0]` to >=82.8, or relocate the GPS antenna. Independently fixable from PCB gerbers, but blocks a working enclosure.

## MAJOR

**M1. LoRa u.FL (ANT1) is mid-board under the panel deck, not at a case-wall edge.**
ANT1 at board x126, y67.5 sits ~4mm inside the Cmts.User deck rect (x122..181.2), under the panel module. This buys the excellent 1.8mm RF_ANT trace but violates "u.FL at case-wall edge" (`DESIGN.md:245`). The LoRa ANT pin faces the board interior and the left edge is occupied by the WROOM, so there is no clean short-trace edge spot. The mated MHF1 + coax must fit the 4mm deck gap, be plugged, and route out from under the display; the enclosure review did not check this. **Fix:** confirm mated-connector clearance under the deck and a coax exit channel, or treat as a placement redesign.

**M2. USB pair is half-routed, unclassed, and the ESD is 10.6mm from the connector.**
`/power/USB_DP` and `/power/USB_DM` (U101→U401) have 0.00mm track (unrouted, part of the 198 ratsnest). The raw pair is on `Default` netclass — no USB class exists in `pager.kicad_pro` — which is the root of the 12 clearance DRC violations (D+/D- pads at 0.2mm, DP_RAW track to DM_RAW via at 0.15mm). U101 (138.5,92) is 10.6mm from USBC1 (148,96.8) vs the "within 5mm" spec; raw legs mismatch (DP_RAW 21.6mm vs DM_RAW 16.4mm). Functionally tolerant at Full-Speed native USB, and 90-ohm edge-coupled is impractical on 2-layer 1.6mm anyway, but the "locked pair" is neither impedance-defined nor DRC-clean. **Fix:** finish the U101→U401 route matched, add a USB netclass, move U101 within 5mm, add a receptacle clearance exception so DRC reaches 0/0/0.

**M3. The 1mm-under-glass keepout is not separately enforced.**
L301 (2.6mm), D101 and D402 (SMA, 2.3mm) sit inside the deck rect. Only the 3.2mm deck rule is marked (they pass it); the tighter invariant "nothing taller than 1mm under the panel glass" (`DESIGN.md:244`) has no glass sub-region drawn or checked. **Fix:** add the active-glass rectangle as a keepout and confirm the boost cluster is under the module's non-glass margin.

**M4. (Enclosure) Battery Z-stack has ~0.5mm swell margin, loading toward the glass.**
`scad:58-59,191-200`. `BAT_H=6.5`, `BAT_GAP=0.5`; `cavity_h == stack_h` (12.6==12.6), passing the assert only by epsilon. A swelling LiPo bows the PCB toward the module. **Fix:** `BAT_GAP -> 1.0` or raise `GLASS_GAP`.

## MINOR

- **COMPONENTS.md D201 anode/cathode reversed (doc only).** Line 68 says "GNSS_VBAT (A), +3V3 (K)" and "cathode band = +3V3 side"; the netlist has anode=+3V3, cathode=GNSS_VBAT (correct — 3V3 feeds the keep-alive). Schematic is right; the doc describes the broken orientation. Fix the text.
- **DRC not at the 0/0/0 gate:** 36 violations (13 silk-over-copper, 7 silk-overlap, 4 silk-edge-clearance clipping USBC1/U401 refdes, 12 clearance). Also `GNSS_VCCRF`, all `USB_D*`, and EPD rails VSL/VSH1/VSH2/VCOM/VDD match no netclass pattern, so the "netclass audit vs hierarchical names" invariant (`README.md:99`) is not yet met.
- **Display window centered on module, not active area** (enclosure M3, `scad:118-124,201`): 1.25mm short-axis margin; the GDEY active area is offset by the FPC tail. Bias the window to the panel drawing.
- **No test points or fiducials** for a first-article PCBA with 0.5mm FFC + QFN GPS + WROOM. Add global fiducials and TPs on 3V3/VSYS/GND/VBAT_SENSE/UART.
- **WROOM decoupling minimal** (10uF + 100nF only); Espressif wants more distributed 100nF.
- **Firmware (fleet, not a pager defect):** only `bramble_pager.h` sets `radio_dio2_rf_switch`; `heltec_v4.h`/`heltec_v3.h`/`tdeck_plus.h` use TCXO_DIO3 but never set the flag — run the DIO2 A/B RSSI test on V4 and T-Deck too. GNSS enable pin is a hardcoded literal (`gps.c:224,296`), but this mirrors the existing heltec_v4 pattern.
- **Enclosure envelope doc mismatch:** computed case 100.8 x 54.8 x 16.4mm vs `DESIGN.md:262` "~104x65x18mm". Update the doc.

## Verified correct (independently confirmed)

Schematic parity 0; 82 parts, no floating nets. **Pin map is a three-way match** — DESIGN.md ↔ schematic netlist (I checked all 26 GPIO assignments on U401) ↔ firmware header (fork confirmed 100%, zero mismatches; GNSS TX/RX not crossed, USB D+/D- not swapped). Load-share P-FET body diode blocks VSYS→BAT when USB present, passes cell→VSYS when absent; dead-battery USB boot works via D101. Battery sense taps BAT_PLUS at factor 2 (matches firmware `divider_factor=2`). CC1/CC2 separate 5.1k. TP4056 TEMP-to-GND disable, CE/LDO-CE ties, buzzer/vibra flyback across the coils, GNSS gate polarity (R201 pullup, GPIO38 LOW=on) and ON/OFF pullup to the switched rail, DIO3-NC / DIO2-internal radio wiring — all correct. **FS8205A nonstandard pinout** (1=S1/2=D/3=S2/4=G2/5=D/6=G1) confirmed against the datasheet pin figure and matches the netlist. **All 24 EPD connector pins** verified against the mirror (connector N = panel 25-N). **EPD boost/charge-pump** matches GoodDisplay p29 exactly (three Schottky orientations, 2.2Ω kelvin RESE, 47uH, 4.7uF flying cap, eight 1uF rail caps). **GNSS bias tee** matches the ATGM336H reference circuit (no missing DC block). **Firmware:** 0x9D SetDio2AsRfSwitchCtrl issued in STDBY before any TX, TCXO=2.7V, GPS gate driven LOW on init / HIGH on deinit with the R201 pullup holding it off at cold boot, USB-Serial-JTAG console set for N8R2.
